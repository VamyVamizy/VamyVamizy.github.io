Game Guide

Quick Start Tips

Pay attention to suspicious blocks and room details, since many hidden treasures are tucked away in secret spots. Finding these early can speed up your progress a lot.

Some hidden interactions only work when certain party members are with you, so revisit areas as your team grows.

Important Places

The Market Stall is where you stock up before harder fights. You can buy healing food, buffs, and even loot boxes for weapon upgrades and useful surprise items.

Some foods grant elemental resistance and damage boosts, which can make difficult battles much easier.
